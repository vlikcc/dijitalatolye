## Direction contract
THESIS: A working discovery atlas with game content visible immediately and subject colour linking the catalogue, the game and its teaching note.
OWN-WORLD: Neutral paper (#F6F7F9) catalogue and shared header, white controls, navy (#24344D) text/actions/brand, and muted blue-grey (#606A79) support text. Hover is deep navy (#17263C), selected fill pale blue-grey (#E8EDF4), dividers/outline borders (#DDE2E8), input/scrollbar stroke (#8A94A4), and focus (#3B5F8C). Subject colours, logo pixels and game art stay intact. Manrope 750 discovery headings and 800 brand name; Source Sans 3 controls. Teacher and detail/player surfaces retain their existing root tokens. The light palette serves daylight classrooms and home study.
STORY: Choose a subject or search, inspect the real game scene, play a short activity, then save or copy its link. A teacher can read a practical note and reach the existing upload flow.
FIRST VIEWPORT: A 76px header (64px on phones) shares one continuous neutral paper layer with discovery; it is transparent here and solid neutral elsewhere. The existing single line-to-colour puzzle signature uses navy strokes, with grouped Oyunlar / Kaydettiklerim navigation. Below 1100px a labelled Menü exposes every destination. At 1024px and above the 32px Manrope 750 heading “Oynadıkça yerine oturur.” sits left of search; narrower views stack them, with 28px phone type, line-height 1.25 and tracking -0.03em. The supporting sentence remains “Matematikten Türkçeye, sınıfına uygun bir oyun seç.” The two-row wordmark keeps a Manrope 750 descriptor and 800 name at 28/23/21px. A shared immediate grade/subject strip precedes the centred result label/count and cards. Below 768px both filters are always-visible labelled selects. The non-interactive body paper layer sits behind text and opaque controls: a 512×300px repeat, grayscale(1) brightness(0.535) contrast(16), multiply at 5% opacity, solid mask through 45% then fading to its end. Height is 334px by default, 450px up to 1023px, 394px up to 600px, and 455px up to 360px, ending at the first cards. No colour gradient, puzzle gutter decoration, gamepad pattern or new motion. At 1440×900 the first four cards fit fully; at 390×844 the first image and title are visible without scrolling. Contextual filtered/saved headings remain unchanged. At 1024px and above, the unfiltered introduction includes up to three compact real catalogue covers beside its copy; they are hidden below 1024px and omitted for filtered, saved or empty results. No featured panel or intro CTA is added. The detail heading and teaching information follow the scoped frontend extension.
FORM: Grounded candidate 6, a contemporary colour-coded school atlas; chosen explicitly by the user. The installed engine returned an API-backed Operate direction roll during planning, but does not emit the newer seed-reference field. Raw replay key retained only in local session, not committed. User-pinned colours, logo and standard controls win over reference materials. Signature: selecting a subject connects the same colour and label across discovery, game metadata and the teaching note; no ornamental map UI. Reduced motion removes transitions.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Frontend composition evidence

The cover trio is decorative and hidden from assistive technology. Each cover occupies a contained 70×70px frame; adjacent covers overlap by 30px and the middle cover sits 20px lower. Selection uses existing public catalogue covers, prefers distinct subjects, avoids duplicate cover URLs and skips missing covers. These images do not replace the searchable card list or introduce fabricated fallback art.

Cards retain real cover, subject, grade, optional duration and Demo/Yayımlandı metadata. Titles and metadata wrap, the direction arrow keeps its width, and the bottom row aligns through the card content layout. Save remains a separate 44px control. Hover/focus changes only border and title colour; reduced motion removes the new short transitions. Related games use this same card.
